# Team and Permissions

## Workspace Membership Model

Seats are workspace members under the inviter's subscription.

- Invite accepted -> user created/attached to inviter workspace.
- Invitee inherits inviter plan context.
- Invitee does not choose a separate plan.

## Roles

- Viewer: read-focused access.
- Moderator: moderation and operational controls.
- Admin: broader workspace management rights.
- Owner: highest authority, including admin-user management policies.

## Seat Enforcement

- Seat usage is enforced by plan entitlements.
- Free/Standard/Pro cap seat counts differently.
- Owner/admin users can monitor seat usage in Team views.
