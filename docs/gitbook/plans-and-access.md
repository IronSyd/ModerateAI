# Plans and Access

## Plan Model

### Free

- Self-serve activation.
- 20 AI responses/day.
- 1 seat.
- Up to 1 Telegram group, 1 Discord server, 1 website destination.
- 7-day history.
- 10MB knowledge base limit.

### Standard

- Request-only (Contact Support), then admin activation.
- 2,500 AI responses/day.
- Up to 5 seats.
- Up to 3 destinations per platform type.
- 90-day history.
- 250MB knowledge base limit.

### Pro

- Request-only (Contact Support), then admin activation.
- 6,000 AI responses/day.
- Up to 20 seats.
- Up to 10 destinations per platform type.
- 365-day history.
- 1,000MB knowledge base limit.

## Contact Support Flow

- Logged-in users can click `Contact Support` from plan pages.
- Logged-out users are prompted to sign in/create an account before support contact can continue.
- Admin/owner confirms payment and activates plan in Admin -> Users.

## Bot Ownership by Plan

- App-owned bots are the default onboarding path for Telegram and Discord.
- Pro can switch Telegram and Discord independently to `BYOB` (bring your own bot).
- Free and Standard cannot enable BYOB mode.

## Access States

Accounts can be set by admin actions:

- allowed/active
- suspended (billing restriction)
- deactivated
- banned

When a workspace owner is restricted, access restrictions propagate to invited team members in that workspace.
