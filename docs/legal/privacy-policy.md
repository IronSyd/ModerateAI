# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **ModerateAI** ("ModerateAI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, dashboard application, website widget, and connected integrations (including Telegram and Discord bots).

If you use ModerateAI on behalf of an organization, workspace, or business, this policy applies to information processed as part of those services, subject to any separate contractual terms you may have with us.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- the ModerateAI marketing website and public pages
- the ModerateAI web application and workspace dashboard
- the ModerateAI website widget and lead capture experiences
- Telegram and Discord bot interactions powered by ModerateAI
- customer support, admin operations, and account management workflows

It does not apply to third-party services that you connect to or visit directly (for example, Telegram, Discord, or Stripe), except to the extent we receive data from or send data to those services in order to provide ModerateAI functionality.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ModerateAI, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account and Workspace Information

- name, email address, and account credentials or authentication metadata
- workspace role and permissions (for example, viewer, moderator, admin, owner)
- workspace settings, integration settings, and configuration preferences
- billing and plan information (if applicable)

### 2.2 Conversation and Message Data

We process and store conversation content to provide AI responses, moderation, analytics, and operational tools.

This may include:

- website widget chat messages
- Telegram bot messages in supported chats/groups
- Discord bot messages in supported channels/servers
- AI-generated responses and moderation-related actions
- message metadata (timestamps, conversation IDs, channel/chat IDs, and platform identifiers)

### 2.3 Website Widget and Lead Capture Data

When website lead capture is enabled, we may collect:

- visitor-provided contact details (for example, name, email, company)
- lead messages and conversation context
- widget session identifiers and related technical metadata

### 2.4 Knowledge Base Content and Imported Sources

If you upload or import content into a ModerateAI knowledge base, we may process and store:

- uploaded documents (for example, `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`)
- content extracted from URLs or crawled pages
- knowledge base metadata (titles, source URLs, tags, sync status, and related metadata)

You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights and permissions to upload or import content into the service.

### 2.5 Usage, Device, and Technical Data

We may collect technical and operational data such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from IP (where applicable)
- browser and device information
- session cookies and session identifiers
- request logs, error logs, and diagnostics data
- feature usage telemetry (for example, admin operations, sync runs, and system events)

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- provide, operate, and maintain ModerateAI
- deliver AI-powered responses, moderation, and workflow automation
- route widget conversations to the correct knowledge base/domain configuration
- support Telegram and Discord bot integrations
- process and manage leads captured through the website widget
- enable conversation review, correction, and approved learning workflows
- improve product quality, reliability, and safety
- monitor system performance, detect abuse, and troubleshoot issues
- provide customer support and admin operations features
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies

## 4. AI and Automated Processing

ModerateAI uses AI and automated systems to generate responses, assist with moderation, and support workflow automation.

This includes:

- AI-generated responses to questions and inquiries
- automated moderation checks and moderation-related actions (subject to your configuration)
- AI-assisted processing of imported or uploaded content for knowledge retrieval workflows

AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable in some contexts. Customers remain responsible for reviewing and configuring the service appropriately for their use case.

ModerateAI may also support human review and correction workflows (for example, admin corrections and explicit approval for learning) to improve future responses.

## 5. Knowledge Base and User-Provided Content

ModerateAI allows workspace administrators to upload documents and import content from URLs into knowledge bases.

We process this content to:

- store it in your workspace
- support retrieval and grounding of AI responses
- maintain sync state for URL-based imports (including stale status for removed pages, where applicable)

You represent that you have all necessary rights to upload, import, and process this content through ModerateAI.

## 6. Integrations and Platform Data (Telegram / Discord / Website)

When you connect integrations:

- Telegram and Discord messages may be processed to provide configured bot responses, moderation actions, and analytics
- website widget interactions may be processed to provide AI chat responses and optional lead capture
- platform identifiers and metadata may be stored to support routing, configuration, and reporting

Data shared with Telegram, Discord, or other connected services is also subject to those services’ own privacy policies and terms.

## 7. Cookies, Sessions, and Local Storage

ModerateAI uses cookies and similar technologies to support:

- authentication and session management
- security and fraud/abuse prevention
- user experience and application continuity

The ModerateAI website widget may also use browser storage (such as local storage) for session continuity and user experience. If browser privacy settings block local storage, the widget may use degraded or in-memory fallback behavior.

We do not state that a cookie consent banner is provided unless and until it is actually implemented.

We may share information with service providers and subprocessors that help us operate ModerateAI, such as providers for:

- AI model processing
- cloud hosting and infrastructure
- database or storage infrastructure
- email delivery and notifications
- billing and payments
- analytics, monitoring, and error reporting

Examples may include providers such as OpenAI, hosting providers, database providers, Stripe, SendGrid, Telegram, and Discord, depending on the features you use and how your deployment is configured.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of ModerateAI, our users, or others.

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of "sell" unless explicitly disclosed otherwise.

Retention may vary by data type and plan level. For example, conversation history retention in ModerateAI may differ by workspace plan (such as Free, Standard, and Pro tiers).

We may retain certain logs, audit records, and operational telemetry longer where needed for security, compliance, or troubleshooting.
